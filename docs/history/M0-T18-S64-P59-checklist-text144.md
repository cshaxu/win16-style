# M0 T18 S64 P59: 144-DPI VB checklist text and frame scope

At 144 DPI P55/P57 replaced the observed runtime checkbox bitmap but the VB5/VB6 checklist owner scope remained limited to 96 DPI. Consequently the real VB5 sample still showed its native text and scrollbar. The exact VB runtime-owned fixed owner-draw multi-select checklist role now reuses the established text and frame scope at 144 DPI too. The native runtime remains responsible for items, measurement, selection, notifications, and application font properties; other DPI values and roles retain their previous behavior. The bitmap rule remains separately constrained by its observed source and item geometry. Original resource headers and provenance are byte-identical.

At 144 DPI the private DPI-aware real x86 VB5 sample used MSVBVM50 and the final package DLL. Its four captures were byte-identical to the prototype: relative to the previous package, the first-row text area had 1,607 changed pixels while each 11x11 original checkbox area had zero changed pixels. Mouse and keyboard selection restored 0 to 1 to 0, and the process/job exited normally. The real 96-DPI sample had byte-identical previous/prototype before, checked and restored captures and normal exit. These are the observed samples, not all layouts or language combinations.

A real x86 VB6 CBMXfer viewer checklist was actually opened at 144 DPI with the final package, contained two private test items, changed selection 0 to 1, and matched the prototype before/after bitmaps. The observer explicitly closed its self-owned hidden Options form, then both target and job exited; this is not proof of unassisted viewer shutdown. Correction to P57: its reported VB6 final-package run kept the viewer-skip environment switch and therefore verified the application's main window/exit, not the viewer checklist. P59 removes the switch and supplies the missing actual viewer evidence.

The self-contained 144-DPI model now verifies its source HFONT is preserved, styled Latin text differs after removing the scope, and Chinese and mixed text preserve their native fallback across scope removal. Four VB5/VB6 and x86/x64 combinations passed; the old package failed all four at the missing checklist attachment, providing a negative control. An initial test incorrectly expected bitmap replacement to disappear on attachment removal; bitmap interception is independent, so that assertion was corrected before the successful runs. The actual final package passed 16 focused cases including both 96-DPI models, all four 144-DPI models, and eight mandatory baseline cases with no skips in 57.11 seconds at two jobs. Both installed locations passed the x86/x64 VB5 144-DPI model.

Clean C17 MinGW GCC/Ninja/ccache dual-width build, ABI, source manifest and original-resource checks passed. Production diff: control_class.h adds 2 lines and removes 1; its runtime and paint owners remain unchanged. Test source adds 27 and removes 4; the package runner adds 4 and removes 4 marker lines. The released hashes, identical in both destinations, are run16s.exe `b8148902eeaea18226e46f1bf083564f15c0a4568a89d9b0332bdd8b91043251`, ntw16s32.dll `68282e8ce01f1f9980e00ae173cebd4ee90d61feb223404ea44c6ebf4b4b5d63`, ntw16s64.dll `f4724e8a78e607180ea303b3de9a7888a07391b977ca08bc565dffcb95de25d2`, demo.exe `a32bd829f91327bab9a73e2855471c0d0b4048dbe98d49645e6378ba33af4edc`.
